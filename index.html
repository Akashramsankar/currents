How to play

Goal: Today's clue has a hidden answer. Slide the grid until the answer's letters click into one unbroken line — Currents will complete the circuit automatically.
Read the clue. The blanks below it show how many letters each word has, e.g. (4, 5).
Shift the grid. Swipe any row or column — or tap the edge arrows — to slide letters. They wrap around the edges. Each shift counts as one move.
Follow the sparks. A glowing line appears between two neighbouring tiles when they're next to each other in the answer. Its arrow points from the earlier letter to the next, showing which way the phrase flows. A violet spark means that link jumps from the end of one word to the start of the next. Use them to line up the whole phrase into one connected chain. Repeated letters? Sparks mark only the correct copy — if a letter sits next to the right neighbour but stays dark, it's the wrong one for that spot.
Complete the circuit. Once every answer letter connects in order, the board auto-completes with a glow. No extra tracing step needed.
Stuck on the moves? The Hint button glows the row or column to slide next — and the arrow shows which way to push it.
Beat Par. Par is the target number of moves for the day, set a little above the best possible solve while Currents is still tough. Finish under it for a Birdie or Eagle. The ★ stars by the clue rate how tough today's puzzle is.
